# DEC-002 — Autopilot mandate — checkout, from G5

## Decision

From G5, the agent decides for the owner everything a skill would otherwise ask, and records each
decision in the ledger. Parameters — see `decisions.yaml`.

## Why

The owner wants to review the finished application, not fourteen intermediate questions.

## Cost

A decision the owner would have taken differently is found at review time, and costs a superseding
`DEC-` rather than a sentence in the room.
